Privacy Policy

ClipAgent, by NxNe Studios. Last updated 31 October 2026.

What ClipAgent is

ClipAgent is a private tool used only by NxNe Studios. It records gameplay of our own Roblox game, Verity: Ashes of the Lost, edits it into short videos, and uploads them to NxNe Studios' own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (Google sign-in, scope youtube.upload): used only to upload our videos to our own channel.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish): used only to read the basic account info TikTok requires before posting, and to upload our videos to our own account.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the studio's own computer and are never sent anywhere except back to Google or TikTok to upload videos. ClipAgent does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access anyone else's accounts.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings.
